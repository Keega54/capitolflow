How long the public waited

Days between the trade and its disclosure. The STOCK Act allows 30 days from notification and 45 from the trade; bars past that line are late filings.

Clustered trading

Windows where an unusual number of separate people traded the same stock the same direction. Co-occurrence, not proof of anything — but it is where a human should look first.

Traders, ranked

Volume, filing punctuality, and timing accuracy. Accuracy is excess return versus the benchmark over 90 days from the trade date, shrunk toward the population average in proportion to sample size — so a handful of lucky trades does not top the table.

Lobbying overlay

Companies by federal lobbying spend against congressional trading interest in the same quarter. A relationship here is co-occurrence and nothing stronger.

Sources: U.S. House Clerk financial disclosures, U.S. Senate Electronic Financial Disclosure, Senate/House Lobbying Disclosure Act filings.
